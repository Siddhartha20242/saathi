Try Saathi

No extension needed. This page stands in for the Chrome extension, using the same code.
How to try this (about two minutes)
  1. On the right, press Start a new group. Open Show the way, name a path and press Start.
  2. On the left, use the practice website: press Pay your bill, click the boxes, press Check bill, then Pay now. Press Finish and save.
  3. Choose Labels renamed below. On the Follow tab, press Start. It finds the buttons even though their names changed.
  4. Switch the language, or open Get help, press Ask my helper and open the helper page on a phone.

The practice website is made up. Nothing here is real and nothing is paid. The on-device model needs desktop Chrome; without it, everything except the last re-finding step still works.
