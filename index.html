How it works

  1. Your family shows the way once

    A son, a daughter or a neighbour does the task on the real website, one time.

  2. Saathi shows you each step

    You see one step at a time, in big words, read aloud if you like. The right button glows.

  3. You press. Saathi never does.

    You stay in charge. Saathi never types or presses for you, and it never sees your passwords.

Made to be safe

  • It points. You press. It never clicks or types for you.
  • It does not see what you type, and it does not keep your pages.
  • If it is not sure which button to point to, it says so and asks your family.

Keep Saathi on your phone

It works like an app, and it still opens with no internet.

For developers and judges: where Saathi runs

One codebase and one Firebase project. Firebase AI Logic's hybrid inference picks the on-device model or the cloud model on each device, so nothing is built twice.
