Try Saathi

No extension needed. This page stands in for the Chrome extension, using the same code.
  1. On the right, press Start a new group. Then in Show the way once, name a path and press Start.
  2. On the left, use the practice website: press Pay your bill, click the boxes, press Check bill, then Pay now. Press Finish and save on the right.
  3. Choose Labels renamed below, then under Follow a path press Start. It finds the buttons even though their names changed.
  4. Choose a language, or press Ask my helper and open the helper page on a phone.
The practice website is made up. Nothing here is real and nothing is paid. The on-device model needs desktop Chrome; without it, everything except the last re-finding step still works.
